# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior expected of all members of "PKS Java". It exists to promote integrity, respect, and accountability within our community.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all employees, contractors, volunteers, board members, and representatives of "PKS Java" in all work-related settings, including off-site events and digital communications.

## 3. Core Principles

All members are expected to:

- **Act with integrity** — Be honest and transparent in all dealings.
- **Treat others with respect** — Value diverse perspectives and communicate professionally.
- **Take responsibility** — Own your actions and their consequences.
- **Follow the law** — Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
- **Avoid conflicts of interest** — Disclose any personal interests that may conflict with your role.

## 4. Expected Standards of Behavior

- Maintain a professional and inclusive environment.
- Protect confidential information and company property.
- Use organizational resources responsibly and only for legitimate purposes.
- Report suspected violations in good faith.
- Cooperate fully with internal investigations.

## 5. Prohibited Conduct

The following will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind.
- Violence, threats, or intimidation.
- Fraud, theft, bribery, or falsification of records.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential or proprietary information.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Substance abuse that impairs work performance or safety.

## 6. Consequences

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or association, and may be referred to law enforcement where appropriate.

## 7. Acknowledgment

All members are required to read, understand, and acknowledge this Code of Conduct upon joining and annually thereafter.
